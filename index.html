Profile

Design is the center. Everything else helps me see the whole system.

I am most interested in the moment when everyone knows something is not working, but no one can yet say exactly why.

That is where I begin: I listen to people, map the real work, separate symptoms from causes, and turn the findings into a direction users understand and a team can build.

More than 17 years across visual design, web, enterprise UX, business analysis, and technology give me the range to do that.

The specialists knew manufacturing and investigation methods. My task was to understand how they actually applied them and turn that work into a connected digital process.

Fishbone, Is/Is Not, and 5 Whys needed to connect with the investigation: what the user was trying to establish, what information was already available, and what the next decision required.

Through research with specialists, process mapping, and collaboration with Product Owners and engineers, I connected user needs with the system's capabilities. I supported project planning, feasibility assessment, and shaping the direction before detailed design.

The Figma example shows how that approach carries into the product: guidance is available within the task, methods have clear entry points, and users retain an overview of the rest of the investigation.

How I work

Tiny Humans Engine

Work-in-progress 2D engine and editor prototype developed through AI-assisted collaboration

Tiny Humans is a 30,000+ line personal prototype with an editor, runtime, asset pipeline, scripting system, and packaging work. I used AI tools within a structured loop: Specify -> Architect -> Govern -> Validate. The project demonstrates how I define requirements, manage dependencies, test persistence, and document decisions. It is not a commercially released or production product.

SDK & Editor Progress

The prototype contains 29 major systems, but the broader editor and SDK concept is approximately 15% complete. The proof of concept below is a small extract of about 1,800 lines.

System Architecture

Engine Registry + Event Bus pattern decouples 29 systems. Modular design means any system (lighting, audio, NPCs, scripting) can be developed, tested, and updated independently.

Registry Pattern Event Bus 29 Systems

Validation & Guardrails

A Save Validator checks seven categories of data integrity. A Feature Manifest reduces duplicate work, while persistence checklists support write, read, and round-trip verification.

Save Validator Feature Manifest Round-trip Testing

AI Governance

Project-specific AI instructions define persistence checks, common bug prevention, architecture rules, and implementation workflows. Outputs are reviewed and tested rather than accepted automatically.

Copilot Instructions Bug Prevention Architecture Rules

Implemented Prototype Systems

Ray-cast lighting with DDA shadows. AI state machines with faction targeting. 4-channel spatial audio. Scene management with map caching. 8-type logic gates with loop prevention. A full scripting SDK with Runtime API.

Lighting & Shadows NPC AI / FSM Audio Engine SDK

Working Method: Structured AI-Assisted Development

01

Specify

Define requirements with the precision of an RFP - scope, constraints, success criteria, integration points.

02

Architect

Design the system before writing code. Registry patterns, event flows, data models, persistence paths.

03

Govern

Copilot instructions, feature manifests, validation checklists. The AI follows rules, not vibes.

04

Validate

Save validators, round-trip testing, documented decisions. Every change is traceable and defensible.

This project does not replace professional engineering experience. It shows how I use structured AI collaboration, verification, and documentation to explore a technically complex personal idea.

It is supporting evidence of systems thinking, persistence, and hands-on prototyping.
