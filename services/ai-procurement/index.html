Working method

How I approach the work

A structured approach connecting the problem, the people involved, and practical implementation.

01

Frame

Clarify the business problem, existing systems, decision criteria, and regulatory constraints.

02

Investigate

Gather public evidence and work with specialists to distinguish documented capabilities from unsupported claims.

03

Evaluate

Compare options, integration feasibility, risks, dependencies, and potential changes to existing systems.

04

Support decisions

Communicate feasible next steps and make unresolved questions and follow-up work explicit.

Data Protection & GDPR

Questions about data flows, hosting, access, and third parties, considered alongside specialist privacy and security input.

GxP & Regulatory Fit

Validation needs, traceability, change control, and how a proposed tool fits regulated pharmaceutical workflows.

Technical Claims vs. Reality

Reviewing evidence with engineers and specialists to assess whether an integration is feasible or a wider system change is needed.
